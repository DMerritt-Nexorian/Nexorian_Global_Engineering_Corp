## 1. INFORMATION WE COLLECT
We collect minimal personal information required to deliver software entitlements, process payments, and protect system security:
- **Account & Purchase Data:** Name, email address, company name, and transaction details via secure merchant payment processors.
- **Technical & Security Logs:** IP address, browser type, download access tokens, and WAF telemetry for security auditing and abuse prevention.

## 2. HOW WE USE INFORMATION
- To generate digital software licenses and deliver downloadable release artifacts.
- To verify payment integrity and enforce license entitlements.
- To maintain security, detect threats, and protect intellectual property.

## 3. DATA SHARING & THIRD PARTIES
We do NOT sell, rent, or trade customer personal data. Data is shared strictly with essential service providers (e.g. payment processors like Stripe/Paddle, infrastructure hosting providers) solely to execute transactions and deliver services.
