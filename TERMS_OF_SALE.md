## 2. REFUND POLICY FOR DIGITAL DOWNLOADS
Due to the digital nature of downloadable software, source code, and cryptographic SDKs:
- **Standard Software & SDKs:** Refund requests submitted within fourteen (14) days of purchase may be granted if the software has not been activated, or in the event of verified technical defects that cannot be remediated by support.
- **Enterprise & Source Licenses:** Fees for custom enterprise contracts, custom RTL IP, and source-available licenses are non-refundable once source access or signing keys have been delivered.

## 3. REVOCATION OF LICENSE UPON REFUND
If a refund is issued, the associated license key and digital entitlement are immediately revoked, disabling future download access and software updates.
